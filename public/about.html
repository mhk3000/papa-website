About

I am Hunaid Khorakiwala, a poet, businessman, and thinker, deeply committed to my faith and community. My website showcases my work and contributions in various fields, including poetry, religious scholarship, and social commentary.

Mission

My goal is to help make significant Islamic texts more accessible to a broader audience. I work on translating and sharing essential works like Nahjul Balagha so that English-speaking readers can benefit from their teachings.

Values

I believe in the importance of ethical behavior in both business and personal life. Transparency, fairness, and respectful treatment of all—customers, employees, and investors—are central to my approach.

Contributions

I focus on documenting and sharing religious and cultural insights in a way that can inspire and educate. My writings and translations aim to encourage a deeper understanding of Islamic heritage and principles.

Future Vision

I seek ways to bridge cultural and religious gaps, making timeless wisdom available to all. Ethical practices and community service guide my efforts.
